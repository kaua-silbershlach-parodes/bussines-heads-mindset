# Interesting facts about business

![Interesting facts about business](../images/002-Interesting-facts-about-business-12-14.jpg)

1. According to statistics, 90% of restaurants close in the first year after opening.

2. Tuesday is the most productive day of the workweek.

3. Approximately 33 new products appear in the world every day, 13 of which are toys.

4. Every day, about 65 people become millionaires.

5. Coffee is the second best-selling product in the world after gasoline.

6. Approximately 80% of all toys in the world are produced in China.

7. McDonald's main revenue comes from beverages, regardless of the country's location.

8. Children's products in stores are placed on the floor so that children can notice them.

9. About 20% of millionaires have never received higher education.

10. American Airlines was able to save about $40,000 by simply removing one olive from the salads served to business class passengers.

11. In most cases, pleasant smells in stores are artificial and created by air fresheners to stimulate shoppers.

12. Fast food restaurants often play fast music to encourage customers to eat quickly and free up space for new customers.

13. Google founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin offered to sell Google to Yahoo for $1 billion in 1998, but Yahoo refused. After 7 years, in 2005, Google's value was already $80 billion with an annual profit of $1.5 billion.

14. In the 20th century, Russian entrepreneur Nikolay Shustov used an unusual method to promote his brandy in Moscow taverns. He hired people to demand "Shustov brandy" in the taverns, causing scandals and expressing dissatisfaction. Thanks to this campaign, the tavern keepers learned about the new brandy and started buying it.

15. The annual turnover of the advertising market is $1.5 trillion. In comparison, the annual turnover of the tobacco market worldwide is about $400 billion, which is $100 billion less.
