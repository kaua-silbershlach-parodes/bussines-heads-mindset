# How to learn to express your thoughts elegantly?

![How to learn to express your thoughts elegantly?](../images/025-How-to-learn-to-express-your-thoughts-elegantly-12-14.jpg)

There are several recommendations that will help you improve the quality of your speech:

1) Read various literature - newspapers, magazines, as well as our classic domestic literature that you studied in school. Read carefully, consider each sentence. This will help you structure words correctly in a sentence and expand your vocabulary.

2) Avoid using parasitic words in your speech, such as "well", "in general", "sort of", "well, this", "damn", etc. Try to control your emotions in conversation and use simple phrases without haste.

3) Pay attention to the rhythm of speech. Monotonous speech causes boredom. Take pauses and emphasize important moments emotionally, but without exaggeration.

4) Include different metaphors, comparisons and proverbs in your speech. This will bring liveliness to your speech. And of course, don't forget about humor. Joke, including self-irony, if it corresponds to the situation.

5) To practice speech, it is important to have the opportunity to communicate with different people. If such an opportunity is not available, you can use the radio and television. Try to imitate the style of the host: repeat his phrases and copy intonations.

6) There is an interesting exercise. Take any object from your house, for example, a frying pan, and try to describe it using beautiful literary language for 5 minutes. At first, it may be challenging, but it will get easier with each time. Gradually increase the training time and make the topic more complex. Such exercises will help you find the right words and talk about the frying pan for an hour without repeating yourself.

Gradually expand your vocabulary and use this knowledge in conversation. You will notice how words obey you.

Sources of information should be books, articles, newspapers. Study the meanings of unfamiliar words and learn how to pronounce them correctly.

After several months of practice, you will notice that you no longer have trouble expressing your thoughts. You will be able to explain complex things in simple language.
