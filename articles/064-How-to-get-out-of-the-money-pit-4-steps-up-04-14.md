# How to get out of the money pit: 4 steps up

![How to get out of the money pit: 4 steps up](../images/064-How-to-get-out-of-the-money-pit-4-steps-up-04-14.jpg)

**1. Admit that there is a problem.**

Many people are forced to constantly deny themselves everything. But they still run out of money at the most inopportune moment.

Let's face it, you don't know how to handle your money properly.

This skill is not given to us from birth; it needs to be nurtured and constantly cultivated. By themselves, only problems with money can arise.

Financial well-being is always about the efforts you make, wise foresight and a proven sequence of actions.

**2. Check your shopping list.**

The days of scarcity are long gone. Nowadays, there is no need to buy in advance and stock up.

Plan your shopping list so that you become truly rich, and not seem rich (by hanging yourself with shiny tinsel and newfangled gadgets).

Remember that any purchase is a loss of money that you cannot get back. This does not mean at all that you need to become an ascetic monk:

• Allow yourself to enjoy life and enjoy all the benefits of civilization:

• But ostentatious purchases and excessive demonstration of one’s social status/success should be avoided.

**3. Start a piggy bank.**

You can save money on a deposit in a bank. The main thing is to start saving 10-20% of each of your earnings.

This money will become your “financial cushion”. You can spend them in emergency cases: if you have lost your job or need urgent treatment.

You shouldn’t waste this money on unplanned purchases (even if you really want to).

**4. Learn to increase money.**

Choose an investment strategy that suits you. Get into the good habit of buying currencies, stocks and other financial instruments every month.

Don't expect quick results from your investments.

If you are consistent, you will wait for the day when the amount of passive income from your financial capital will exceed the amount of your salary.
