# 10 reasons why simpler is better

![10 reasons why simpler is better](../images/070-10-reasons-why-simpler-is-better-04-14.jpg)

1. When you get rid of chaos in your affairs, life will become clearer and easier to understand and manage.

2. There is more happiness in simple things - in a smile, in enjoying nature, in communicating with family and friends... These are things that we take for granted, but that should be truly appreciated.

3. By reducing chaos, you clear out areas you no longer need to worry about. The less clutter in your life, the calmer you feel and the better you sleep.

4. A simpler life frees up space for activities that energize you.

5. Getting rid of unnecessary things gives you time to be alone with yourself. Regular check-ins with yourself remind you of your priorities and help you maintain simplicity.

6. Simplicity improves work-life balance. Plus your relationship becomes more relaxed and open.

7. Bustiness no longer confuses you among a million priorities, and you become more confident in yourself. Confidence brings a positive outlook on things, which in turn contributes to more effective development.

8. A simple approach to life allows you to take failures philosophically, which means you remain committed to your goals no matter what.

9. The quality of life improves when you don't scatter your attention on everything. Order in your affairs gives you the resources to live life’s moments and eliminates the worry that you are not on time somewhere.

10. What you struggled with will turn out to be easier than you thought. The true reasons for everything that worried you will be revealed to you, and many problems will be resolved by themselves.
