# The power of small steps

![The power of small steps](../images/058-The-power-of-small-steps-04-13.jpg)

We know that you need to set goals and this is a map of development in all areas of life. But keeping your attention on the goal is difficult because it is carried forward in time. What to do in this case? Focus on steps towards the goal!

What are you dreaming about? Make a fortune, travel around the world, win an Oscar for best role in a movie? Such plans for 5, 10, 50 years ahead can be called global goals. They are so large-scale that it seems that even a lifetime is not enough to achieve them. But there are desires that don’t have to take years to achieve—local goals. Sometimes they bring you closer to a global dream or are even part of it and can become a regular procedure for achieving it.

Set small intermediate goals, because small steps work. First you accomplish a small goal, then a larger goal, then an even larger goal, and eventually you achieve what you wanted.

And don't scare yourself! For example, you set a goal to systematically engage in fitness. It often happens like this: you went to the gym a couple of times, then you missed it, then you made a few more attempts, but a little “cooled down” after the break, and then you gave up on it.

I suggest planning like this: this week I will go to training twice. Only twice, and I’m already great! I'll go two more times next week. And in a month I will start planning proper nutrition.

And just like that, without pushing yourself into strict limits, you will get the job done and receive satisfaction.
