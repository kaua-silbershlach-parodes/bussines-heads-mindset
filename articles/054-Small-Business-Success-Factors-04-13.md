# Small Business Success Factors

![Small Business Success Factors](../images/054-Small-Business-Success-Factors-04-13.jpg)

**1. One of the most important factors for success in business is planning. **

This is probably the most important factor. Any business must begin with the development of a plan. Any plan must be as accurate as possible and take into account all possible deviations. Upon closer examination, it becomes clear what is needed for success in business.

It is necessary to analyze the activities of competitors, to identify both positive and negative nuances of their work. When analyzing the work of others, it is worth identifying what you can learn from them, what needs to be improved, and what you can implement in your own business.

**2. Organize work in such a way that each employee feels responsible for their work. **

It is worth distributing your own responsibility among subordinates, supporting the development of your employees in every possible way, involving them in new and new tasks. It is necessary to let your people express themselves; encourage their initiatives if they are useful for the business.

**3. Organize relationships with staff correctly, build trusting relationships with both employees and customers.**

**4. Organization of work. **

When everything in work is debugged to the smallest detail. Everything in the work is distributed among employees. Clearly and specifically.

**5. Competent work with clients. **

Caring for clients, perceiving their opinions, satisfying their needs and desires. The highest level of services provided by your company. The trust of your company's customers is essential. Location of your customers.

**6. Of course, care about your team, about your employees.**

Even when applying for a job, discuss all the requirements and conditions; a written contract is preferable. The agreement will instill confidence in the potential employee. The principle: “Work and reward” should always work. You shouldn’t skimp on professionalism; it definitely pays off. Treat your employees with respect. Pay great attention to their training. The face of your business is your employees. Your success depends on them.

In practice, these factors are rather paths to success that are quite easy to follow. The main thing is to start doing the right thing. There is no point in reinventing the wheel when the principle of its operation has been known to the whole world for a couple of hundred years. And remember, it’s better to use the experience of other people’s mistakes than your own.
