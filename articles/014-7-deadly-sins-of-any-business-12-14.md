# 7 deadly sins of any business

![7 deadly sins of any business](../images/014-7-deadly-sins-of-any-business-12-14.jpg)

1. Irresponsibility: an irresponsible individual is dangerous and uncontrollable, they are no different from someone mentally ill or mentally unhealthy. Trusting them with something is foolish, explaining is like talking to a wall. They are the most dangerous individual in the business.

2. Negligence: work done without willingness, just to get rid of it as soon as possible, will never be of high quality. Therefore, it is meaningless. Negligence is a sign of disrespect towards the work and colleagues.

3. Lack of punctuality: inability to manage one's time leads to consequences that affect the overall result. Everyone starts to suffer. Delaying responsibilities leads to missing deadlines, which is equivalent to not doing the job at all. Being late for meetings, appointments, conferences, shows disrespect towards oneself and colleagues.

4. Weakness of character: fear of taking responsibility, fear of making decisions independently, whining and evading make the individual useless in the business. Such people cannot be trusted, they can let you down in difficult times.

5. Stupidity: an individual who cannot see obvious things, make decisions, and independently complete tasks, becomes irritating to the entire team. Illiteracy and lack of logic are diseases. And sick people should not work.

6. Inadequacy: expressed in various aspects: increased sensitivity to information, diva behavior, conflictiveness, histrionics, inability to control oneself, tendency to spread rumors and discuss people behind their backs. Such individuals are dangerous. They create chaos and confusion.

7. Dishonesty: an individual who deceives, makes promises but does not fulfill them, does not take responsibility for their words, in the end, becomes the cause of many problems. Failure to understand the importance and significance of one's words should lead to a loss of respect and dismissal.
