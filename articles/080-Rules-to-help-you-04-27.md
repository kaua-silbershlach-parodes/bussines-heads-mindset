# Rules to help you

![Rules to help you](../images/080-Rules-to-help-you-04-27.jpg)

1. Try not to impose yourself on people, and then it will become clear whether they really need you.

2. If you love two people at the same time, choose the second one. If you loved the first one, you wouldn't fall in love with the second one.

3. What others think of you should not concern you.

4. Those people who have forgiven most often and endured the longest usually leave unexpectedly and forever.

5. It doesn’t matter if you have no friends; it’s a disaster if they are fake and corrupt.

6. Talk less about your plans.

7. Never forgive cheating. Any betrayal is a comparison, a search for the best. Those who seek the best will never appreciate what they have...

8. Humans are 80% water. If a person has no dreams or goals in life, then he is just a vertical puddle.

9. Don't reject people who want to be with you. They may be the only ones who will stay with you in the most difficult moments of your life.

10. Treat people the way you want, treat them that way! Because there's no way in hell they'll treat you the same way you treat them!
