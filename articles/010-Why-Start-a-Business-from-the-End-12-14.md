# Why Start a Business from the End

![Why Start a Business from the End](../images/010-Why-Start-a-Business-from-the-End-12-14.jpg)

What unites a tightrope walker and an entrepreneur? Both must maintain balance in difficult conditions. However, there is also what sets them apart. This is their attitude towards risk. The path of a tightrope walker is dangerous. And it is hardly possible to find a daredevil who would dare to cross the height without training and without insurance.

But among beginner businessmen, there are plenty of such brave ones. Perhaps, if their life depended on every step of an entrepreneur, businesses would less often fall into the abyss. In business, as in balancing, two principles are important:

- The principle of balance.

- The principle of insurance.

With the right ratio of income and expenses, a business becomes balanced and provides itself with profit. Profit is the balancing pole of your business. Without profit, a business loses stability.

Nothing disciplines like thoughts of profit. You start investing wisely and growing sensibly. But the most important thing: thoughts of profit will lead you to what business starts with - sales.

The volume of sales depends on costs and the profit rate. It is useful to understand what figures are involved at the approach to your business:

- How much will you spend per month on maintaining an office (store, cafe, production): on rent and utilities, banking services, internet and telephony, software and hardware support?

- How much per month will you spend on maintaining employees: on recruiting and training staff, tax payments, and wages?

- How much will you invest monthly in advertising and promoting your business?

The calculations made will give an idea of:

- What markup percentage you need to enter the market and make a profit.

- What sales volume you need for breakeven.

- How many customers you will need to meet the sales plan.

- How many employees will be required to attract/retain this number of customers.

The answers to these questions will convince you that sales are not only a result but also a process that requires constant care. And business should start with them. Uncontrolled sales make business spontaneously unpredictable. No sales - no profit - no business. First, start counting, and then start a business.
