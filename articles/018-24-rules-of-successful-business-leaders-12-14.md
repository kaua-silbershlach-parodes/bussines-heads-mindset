# 24 rules of successful business leaders

![24 rules of successful business leaders](../images/018-24-rules-of-successful-business-leaders-12-14.jpg)

1. Never expect that your customers will be as excited about your product as you are.

2. Provide customers with more value than they paid for, and they will be willing to pay a reasonable price for a longer time.

3. A smile or the ability to remember a customer's name are much more effective sales strategies than discounts or promotions.

4. You exist only because customers have allowed you to exist.

5. The best customer retention strategy is excellent customer service.

6. Your employees should believe that what they do is more important than just making a profit.

7. Customers don't always know what they want, but they always say they do.

8. Even if you have a great idea, it doesn't mean people will automatically buy it. Not at all.

9. Outstanding service is the best way to keep customers tied to you.

10. If you try to sell everything, customers will buy nothing.

11. Trust is easy to lose and hard to earn. Remember that before attempting to get rich quickly.

12. Customers are not interested in what you gain from a transaction. They just want to feel like winners.

13. Be clear. You don't get bonuses for complex and convoluted messages.

14. It's difficult to make a mistake when you're focused on making customers happy.

15. The best marketing in the world is simply another satisfied customer.

16. You don't provide value to the buyer by giving them digital goods and calling them gifts or bonuses.

17. The best sales strategy is a good marketing plan and excellent service.

18. Customers remember only what you have done for them now. Not all the work you have done in the past to maintain the relationship.

19. There is always a new company or a new product ready to make your buyers happy when their happiness is no longer your priority.

20. No free services or parties can eliminate unpleasant impressions of interacting with your company.

21. Despite countless excuses, always remember the principle in customer relationships: "It's my fault."

22. Inspiring customers is not what you do, it's simply the essence of your business.

23. The delight of the buyer depends not only on the type of people you hire, but also on every interaction the buyer has with them being a memorable one.

24. Nothing goes to ruin instantly, and nothing is restored instantly. A business starts to decline when no one is paying attention to bringing it back to normal.
