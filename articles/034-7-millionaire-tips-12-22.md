# 7 millionaire tips

![7 millionaire tips](../images/034-7-millionaire-tips-12-22.jpg)

**1. Stop spending money.**

As an example, Robert Fletcher's experience: "Arriving in Ukraine, I bought a mobile phone for only $50, while I see even an 18-year-old girl with a phone costing from $200 to $500."

**2. Start saving money.**

Ideally save 10 to 50% of your income. The logic is simple: when you don't have money, you don't have the opportunity to take advantage of new business opportunities, also check one of the fundamental laws of money, which states: "Money attracts money."

**3. Invest in real estate and your own home.**

Real estate can be a great investment.

**4. Protect your finances.**

Invest with caution, guarantee and, preferably, without risk.

**5. Invest money with smart, experienced people.**

The best investment partner is someone who already has experience in making money. They can suggest which investments generate more income and which are more reliable (these are not the same thing).

**6. Find new ways for the money you save to earn even more money, and this money to earn even more money.**

That is, the money you save, you should invest again and again. Robert Fletcher's rule: only after you have five sources of income, can you spend 10% on entertainment and big purchases (a car, a new TV, etc.)

**7. Learn.**

You need to learn how to earn more money. Ideally, you should learn in a way that will further increase your income.
