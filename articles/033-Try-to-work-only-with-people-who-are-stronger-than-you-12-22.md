# Try to work only with people who are stronger than you

![Try to work only with people who are stronger than you](../images/033-Try-to-work-only-with-people-who-are-stronger-than-you-12-22.jpg)

This list of success laws was compiled by the famous psychologist Randy Gage.

1. Law of liberation. If you need new shoes, get rid of the old ones. If you need new clothes, organize your wardrobe. You must freely let go of your outdated beliefs.

2. Law of exchange. Be ready to let go of what you have to get what you want.

3. Law of imagination. First, you must see prosperity in your imagination. Create a description of your ideal day and only show it to those you trust. Keep this description handy and reread it in your free time.

4. Law of creativity. A person can achieve prosperity through the energy of their thoughts, intuition, and imagination.

5. Law of giving and receiving. If you give something, it comes back to you tenfold. When you receive blessings, it is important to share them with others. If you have a gift and you do not use it, you offend your Divine essence. To respect your abilities, you must rejoice in your gifts and share them with others. By doing this, you attract even more blessings into your life.

6. Law of tithing. The universe always takes its tithe. It is simply a law of gratitude and support - 10% of everything you have. You never know how your tithe will come back to you. Money is a common manifestation of this. It can also come in the form of reconciling with someone, new friendships, healing, etc.

7. Law of forgiveness. If you can't forgive people, you can't accept your wealth. If your heart is filled with hatred, love cannot dwell in it. You must get rid of the negative feelings that consume you and prevent you from finding peace.
