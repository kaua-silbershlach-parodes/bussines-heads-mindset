# 6 rules for achieving success in life

![6 rules for achieving success in life](../images/004-6-rules-for-achieving-success-in-life-12-14.jpg)

1. Law of Mirror. The people around me reflect my own personality, sometimes even aspects of it that I don't think about. For example, if someone is rude to me, it means that I am also ready to show rudeness in my heart. Therefore, there is no need to be offended by others.

2. Law of Choice. I realize that everything that happens in my life is a result of my own choice, based on my feelings and sensations. Therefore, I have no reason to blame anyone else. The authorship of my destiny belongs only to me.

3. Law of Responsibility. I am willing to take responsibility for my decisions and abandon excuses that do not bring anything.

4. Law of Mistakes. I acknowledge the fallibility of my opinions and judgments. Stubbornly clinging to one's own rightness will eventually lead to personal catastrophe. Those who insist on being right do not see reality.

5. Law of Correspondence. In my life, I have exactly what I deserve and as much as I truly correspond to. This applies to relationships, roles, and financial position. Therefore, all my complaints are meaningless. When I change my attitude towards the world around me, circumstances also change.

6. Law of Presence - "Here and Now". The past is gone, and it no longer exists. The future doesn't exist yet either. The inclination to pity the past leads to depression, worrying about the future breeds anxiety. As long as I am alive, I am immortal. This is a reason to rejoice.
