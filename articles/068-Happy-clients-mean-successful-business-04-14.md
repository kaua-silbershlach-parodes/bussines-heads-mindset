# Happy clients mean successful business

![Happy clients mean successful business](../images/068-Happy-clients-mean-successful-business-04-14.jpg)

First impressions always have a big impact. As an entrepreneur, the first step to providing quality customer service is to provide the same quality of service to your own employees.

Do you treat your secretaries with the same courtesy as you treat a client who has come to sign a contract for a tidy sum? Do you start the day with a friendly “hello” to your colleagues? And are you ready to listen to your subordinates when they need it? The kindness you show in your team will triple and very quickly trickle down to the customer relationship level.

**1. Lead by example.**

Employees very often take their cues from their management. Make sure your managers are aware of how they treat employees. Often it is the “low-level” personnel who are the face of the company, an indicator for the client. And they will bring the positivity coming from management to their communication with consumers.

**2. Provide a warm welcome.**

When a client contacts your company, try to provide a friendly welcome and address him by name. The buyer will appreciate your attitude and will judge your company accordingly. A warm welcome will ensure interest in your business and will clearly encourage the client to do business with your company.

**3. Think long term.**

You can show your gratitude to your customers by sending cards for holidays and birthdays, personal discounts, and so on. This will make your buyer feel special and reduce the chance of a deal falling through by 75 percent.

**4. Presumption of innocence.**

At some point in your business, there may be complaints from customers, whether in person or online. Make sure you respond calmly to even the most violent statements. Give your employees instructions on what to say and how to act in such situations. Your responses to complaints must be consistent and timely. After all, then outsiders will be able to appreciate your company’s attempts to resolve the conflict peacefully even with the most dissatisfied client. And this will definitely play into your hands.

**5. Seek feedback.**

Arrange surveys for your clients and colleagues about your company, in their opinion: whether it copes with its tasks. Make an honest effort to resolve problems or implement suggestions. Maintaining a happy atmosphere among customers and employees is key to a successful business. Feedback does most of the work and saves you time thinking about what can and should be changed.
