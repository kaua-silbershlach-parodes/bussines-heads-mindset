# 6 financial principles that will help you achieve a comfortable life quickly

![6 financial principles that will help you achieve a comfortable life quickly](../images/015-6-financial-principles-that-will-help-you-achieve-a-comfortable-life-quickly-12-14.jpg)

**1. Invest in yourself.**

Education, training, and more. Self-development and learning are reliable paths to success and prosperity.

**2. Consider the rule of three eights.**

It is well known that on average we work 8 hours, sleep 8 hours, and have another 8 hours of personal time between these activities. We give our working hours to someone else, and the hours spent sleeping take care of our bodies. Therefore, the only way to grow and improve our lives is to use these remaining eight hours between work and sleep wisely.

**3. Be cautious when providing loans to relatives and close ones.**

It is possible that someone you know or a relative secretly believes that you are favorable to them and that debt forgiveness is possible after some time. Therefore, it is important to negotiate in advance about the exact repayment terms.

**4. Save more, and you will gain freedom of action and flexibility.**

By starting to save on things that are not important to you, you are not hurting yourself - you are gaining flexibility and freedom of action. You are buying the opportunity to change jobs, move to another place, and prioritize what truly matters.

**5. Always spend 1 minute analyzing expenses.**

This trick is surprisingly simple, but it allows you to control your expenses every day and prevent unnecessary spending due to absent-mindedness. Closer to the midday, spend just one minute checking and reflecting on the financial transactions you have already made. This will help immediately identify problems, stop the flow of expenses, and determine an acceptable level of spending for the rest of the day.

**6. Get rid of toxic thoughts related to money.**

Despite the skepticism of some people, our internal beliefs truly have great power. And it is not magic, but psychology. If you do not believe in your success, you will lose strength and not see the point in taking action, resulting in achieving nothing and constantly confirming your internal beliefs. Try, at least as an experiment, to replace negative attitudes towards money with positive ones and live together with them!
