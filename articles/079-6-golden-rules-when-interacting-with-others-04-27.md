# 6 golden rules when interacting with others

![6 golden rules when interacting with others](../images/079-6-golden-rules-when-interacting-with-others-04-27.jpg)

1. The surest way to win an argument is not to raise your voice, but to ask questions. Those that will allow your opponent to look at his arguments from a new angle and accept your point of view. This allows a person to notice his logical flaws.

2. One way to make a good impression is to call your interlocutor by name, instead of the usual impersonal “YOU.” It will subconsciously begin to seem to him that your attitude towards him is special.

3. Pay attention to what your interlocutor is saying. Great people discuss ideas, ordinary people discuss events, and small people discuss others.

4. If at work you are forced to constantly communicate with a large number of people whom you will never meet later, pay special attention to your appearance. Everyone is greeted by their clothes, so it's good when they match who you are - not the other way around.

5. If you need to convince someone else of something, use nonverbal techniques. One of them looks like this: you are standing, and your interlocutor is sitting next to you. This way his attention will be focused only on you, and the lower angle will force him to subconsciously listen more attentively than usual.

6. If they are going to scold you for something, use, again, nonverbal techniques. Does your boss scold you at a planning meeting? Sit as close to him as possible. It is subconsciously uncomfortable for a person to get angry and speak badly about someone who is sitting very close. His tone will be softer and more delicate.
