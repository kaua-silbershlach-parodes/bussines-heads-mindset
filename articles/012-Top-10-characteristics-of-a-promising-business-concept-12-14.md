# Top 10 characteristics of a promising business concept

![Top 10 characteristics of a promising business concept](../images/012-Top-10-characteristics-of-a-promising-business-concept-12-14.jpg)

**1. The creator is fully passionate about the idea. **

If you are pursuing the idea solely for profit, it makes sense to stop right away. Money can be a good motivator, but you need to be prepared for periodic difficulties in entrepreneurship. To assess your commitment to the idea, ask yourself: Can you imagine yourself working on it ten years from now?

**2. The idea is simple. **

Big ideas grow, they don't appear big right away. There was no moment when Mark Zuckerberg suddenly said, "I will create the biggest social network in the world." All great companies of our time started with something very simple (for example, Google began with organizing library books).

**3. The idea is easily explainable. **

When an entrepreneur starts their business, they often spend a lot of time explaining what they are going to do (the audience can be diverse - from potential clients to potential investors). If the founder cannot explain the essence of their project in 10 words, the chances of success are extremely low. It's worth starting with solving one problem, one product.

**4. One source of income. **

A startup must be very successful to find one powerful source of income, not to mention three or four. Therefore, in the beginning, it is advisable to focus on one source and put all efforts into it.

**5. Few steps to profit. **

The more steps there are before you can start earning income, the more people need to help to make the business stable. However, the path to profit for successful projects is short enough.

**6. Knowing your customers. **

First, it is necessary to carefully study the type of consumer who needs your product. Besides, there is no person you would know better than yourself! Remember how many successful projects were born because the creators wanted to satisfy their own needs.

**7. Knowing the market. **

How can you know market trends and predict the future without becoming an expert in a specific field? If you are not an expert yet, it's time to become one.

**8. A sufficiently extensive market. **

Any market covering less than 10 million people will be quite difficult to capture - it's not worth investing time and effort. It is too difficult to win on a small market - you need to think on a large scale.

**9. An original secret ingredient. **

Are you doing something unique? Are you doing something better than everyone else? Do you know a secret that no one else possesses?

**10. Share your idea. **

It is unlikely that anyone dreams of quitting everything and suddenly devoting their life to realizing your idea. Get feedback from as many people as possible - don't be afraid to share.
