# Professional Marketing as a Guarantee of Success

![Professional Marketing as a Guarantee of Success](../images/040-Professional-Marketing-as-a-Guarantee-of-Success-12-22.jpg)

Professional marketing is a versatile tool that ensures business growth. There is also the concept of amateur self-marketing, which is a factor in the decline in trade activity, which in turn reduces demand.

The main idea of self-marketing is the hypothesis that a product can sell itself. The final product automatically creates demand for itself and the need to purchase it. Therefore, it is logical to assume that such a product can give itself value and generate profits based on its sales.

At the same time, it should be remembered that the demand for such a product will be limited and probably not very high. Marketers note that the need for such a product practically tends to zero, while the demand remains at a minimum acceptable level. Accordingly, with low demand, sales will be low.

The process of self-marketing is quite simple. For example, there is a company that produces a product, and then the end consumer purchases it and begins to use it for specific purposes.

During the use of the product, the consumer experiences positive and negative emotions. These emotions reflect the idea of the product. Thus, the consumer enters into close contact with the idea of the product, starting to evaluate and criticize it. Then, all judgments about the product are transmitted from one consumer to another.

Therefore, this is how the marketing of the product is launched. The first consumer shares impressions and emotions with his surroundings. Usually, a person has about 20-100 people around him. That is, potentially, another 20-100 people can learn about the product.

If the majority of emotions among people are negative, then the product is unlikely to be purchased. In the case of positive emotions, the sales of the discussed product may increase. Thus, self-marketing is achieved when the product promotes itself.

Experts in the field of entrepreneurship note that such a scheme will not work if the main goal of any business is to maximize profits. Hence, it can be said that more detailed marketing is needed to increase sales.

Product marketing does not work because consumer tastes, preferences, income levels, and other indicators change. This means that in the first month, he may be satisfied with the product, but then it will evoke negative emotions.

The problem of self-marketing would be solved if there was only one product on the market, but such a phenomenon does not exist in a market economy. Therefore, entrepreneurs need detailed marketing with the study of consumer preferences, defining the target audience for the product, and pricing policy.
