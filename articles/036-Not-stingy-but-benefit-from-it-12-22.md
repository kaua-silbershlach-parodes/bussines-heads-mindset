# Not stingy, but benefit from it

![Not stingy, but benefit from it](../images/036-Not-stingy-but-benefit-from-it-12-22.jpg)

It is not a secret that our words influence our thoughts and our lives, including the presence or absence of money.

For example, if you say that you are "saving" on buying a flat or a car, you feel like you are depriving yourself of something. And this association is not accidental. The word "saving" is associated with a lack of money.

A financially well-off person does not save on themselves and does not tighten their belt. They use their financial resources rationally. None of the billionaires on the Forbes list uses this term, they replace it with others, because they know that thinking shapes success. Therefore, when you say "I'm saving", you automatically classify yourself as "someone without money". Without money, you are forced to save.

It's a completely different story if you have obtained a car or flat in a BENEFICIAL way. The essence is almost the same, but the word "BENEFICIAL" implies that you have gained some benefit, that is, you have done something good for yourself!

Such a simple play on words can shape your subconscious and your financial future. Our thoughts have tremendous power. And depending on the words or images we reinforce them with, that is the result we get. Try transforming similar words, and you will see pleasant changes that will bring you more benefit than savings!
