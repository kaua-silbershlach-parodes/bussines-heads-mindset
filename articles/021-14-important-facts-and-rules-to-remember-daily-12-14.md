# 14 important facts and rules to remember daily!

![14 important facts and rules to remember daily!](../images/021-14-important-facts-and-rules-to-remember-daily-12-14.jpg)

1. This world and society want you to become an illiterate person! The more illiterate you are, the easier it is to sell you various goods and offer services. The size of the television, as many know, is inversely proportional to the coefficient of human intelligence.

2. Do you still believe that education will make you rich and free? You may not believe it, but the curriculum that we are taught in educational institutions becomes outdated on the first day of your education. In other words, we are taught what is no longer needed. And remember, in educational institutions, they teach you how to work, not how to earn, so obtaining a diploma does not necessarily make you a rich and free person.

3. Read, read, read! Read as much as possible, without a break. Of course, you don't know when you will need this knowledge, but believe me, you will be very well prepared for life's circumstances.

4. Communication with others. If a person avoids another person because they consider them unworthy of conversation, it will be difficult for them to find clients, friends, and work.

5. Shyness. Shyness, strangely enough, is a waste of time. Do not allow your emotions to make decisions for you.

6. Relationships. If something does not work in your relationship with another person, in case of a breakup, that "something" will be the cause 100%.

7. Older people are wise people. Communicating with older people will only benefit every person. These people have lived longer than you, so they have more experience in life's circumstances. This communication is simply necessary for your development, absorb useful information for yourself.

8. Goals in superiority. Find idols, people to admire, and set a goal to surpass them.

9. Young and daring. With age, every person becomes more conservative. If you want to become wealthy, achieve goals and success, engage in risky things right now while you are young and energetic.

10. Money and savings. Do not spend money left and right, save up, set a goal, find your field and save for your beginning. This movement will teach you how to spend money and do business with common sense and for a purpose.

11. Choice. If you are faced with a choice of where to spend money: on experiences or things, it is better to choose experiences because the joy of experiences and memories is worth more than things that do not bring joy over time.

12. Saving. Learn to save, if you learn to save, you will learn to earn!

13. Write down your thoughts. Get yourself a notebook and write down all your brilliant ideas and thoughts there.

14. Dream! Most people are sure that dreams remain dreams, believe and dream, you will succeed!
