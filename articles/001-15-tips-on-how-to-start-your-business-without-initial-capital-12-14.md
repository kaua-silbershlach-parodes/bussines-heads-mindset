# 15 tips on how to start your business without initial capital

![15 tips on how to start your business without initial capital](../images/001-15-tips-on-how-to-start-your-business-without-initial-capital-12-14.jpg)

1. Find a partner who will invest.

2. Negotiate with partners about the share of profits.

3. Start with small deals.

4. Look for an investor or a business angel.

5. Always pay with your hard work, time, and intellect.

6. The best businesses always start without investments.

7. Seek advice from those who started from scratch.

8. Many entrepreneurs have opened businesses by borrowing small funds from relatives.

9. Don't strive for immediate big profits.

10. Don't register a legal entity prematurely.

11. Take products on consignment.

12. Don't consider the possibility of taking a loan.

13. Don't hesitate to negotiate with people.

14. A business that starts from scratch grows several times faster.

15. Any business can be started from a blank slate.
