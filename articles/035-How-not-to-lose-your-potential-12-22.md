# How not to lose your potential

![How not to lose your potential](../images/035-How-not-to-lose-your-potential-12-22.jpg)

Quite often you can hear that some people have great potential. However, not everyone will be able to fulfill it. To do this right, you need to determine your goals and put maximum effort into achieving them.

**Look for motivation.**

If you want to unleash your potential, you need to make a reasonable choice in favor of personal growth. Tell yourself that you are ready to change and develop, it will help you rapidly grow towards your goal. To get additional motivation, you can read various books and attend seminars dedicated to self-development, or communicate with people who share your worldview.

**Set goals.**

Developing potential without a specific goal is meaningless. Focus your attention on something significant to you, on the things you want to achieve in the near future. Perhaps you dream of becoming a successful entrepreneur or a renowned athlete. However, remember that every person is a unique individual, and there are no universal ways to unleash potential. Look for goals that genuinely interest you, that are important to you at present, and that you can achieve in the future.

**Don't give up and work systematically.**

The goal you set for yourself may seem unattainable. You may have doubts about its achievability. Your task is to fight such thoughts and move forward, solving all the tasks that arise before you. By doing so, you will prove to yourself that you are capable of resolving the most challenging problems. For example, if you decide to go to the gym, you may have doubts about whether you will ever be able to lift a 100 kg barbell. Your task is to start small, make regular workouts a habit, and then move forward, gradually increasing the weight. In this way, step by step, you will unleash your potential.

**Record achievements.**

Lack of self-confidence, as well as a lack of motivation, often arise from a person not seeing the dynamics of changes in their achievements. Keep a diary or open a blog on the internet. Write down all your successes there, regardless of how significant they are. For example, "Did 50 push-ups", "Attracted the first 10 clients", or "Lost 10 kg" and so on. Every time you have doubts about the correctness of your actions, reread your notes, remind yourself that you have already achieved great results.

**Set an example for yourself.**

When developing your potential, never compare yourself to other people. It is pointless, as there will always be those who have greater successes in the same field, as well as those who lag behind you. Focus on your abilities, set new goals, and continue to develop, without paying attention to others.
