# 22 Daily Actions That Promote Personal Growth

![22 Daily Actions That Promote Personal Growth](../images/082-22-Daily-Actions-That-Promote-Personal-Growth-04-27.jpg)

1. Be more observant. Keep track of everything that happens around you. The life around us is an excellent source for new experiences and new ideas.

2. Don't miss any idea that comes to your mind. Write down each one - even if you can’t implement it right away, you can come back to it later.

3. Take more from the people around you. There is a cycle of examples, ideas, words, relationships, mistakes, experience around you. Notice everything and use it to your advantage.

4. Always carry a book or magazine with you. In this way, you use to your advantage any period of time that could previously have been wasted - time on the road, queues, waiting for results.

5. Read at least 30 minutes every day.

6. Meditate. Make it a habit. This is both “brain gymnastics” and an excellent opportunity to bring clarity to your actions.

7. Try to analyze the past day every evening. What did you succeed and what didn’t? How did you have to make sure everything turned out perfect?

8. Drink more spring water.

9. Do more, think less.

10. Read collections of quotes often - this is a concentrated collection of wisdom.

11. Choose a “useful tip of the day” and apply it.

12. Keep personal “accounting”: write down all income and expenses. So at the end of the month the question does not arise: “Where did I manage to spend all the money?” You can track useless purchases that eat up your budget and avoid them: you can clearly imagine your real purchasing power.

13. Do something for the first time, or find a new way to do something mundane - for example, a more convenient and shorter way to work. It trains your mental abilities.

14. Read online materials with benefit, draw conclusions, and don’t just skim through them.

15. Use a timer more often when performing any task.

16. Learn to use guidelines, algorithms and techniques. Use the known ones, look for new ones. For example, master the hotkey editor - Keyboard Shortcut Editor, with which you can create your own and edit existing hotkeys.

17. Set goals, major and minor, immediate and long-term.

18. Wake up earlier every day.

19. Listen to educational or motivational programs if you are busy doing something that does not require full concentration.

20. Be friendly throughout the day - it inspires optimism and lifts your spirits.

21. Read a random Wikipedia article every day to get information about things you didn’t know about before.

22. Look for something funny or fun in life. This is the only way to truly enjoy it.
