# 25 highly useful tips for success

![25 highly useful tips for success](../images/031-25-highly-useful-tips-for-success-12-22.jpg)

1) Surround yourself with people who uplift your soul. Life is already filled with those who want to drag you down.

2) Practice today what others don't want to do, and tomorrow you will live as others cannot.

3) When you find someone with whom you can build a relationship as freely as being alone in front of a mirror, treasure them like fresh air.

4) Even if they say your train has already left, remember that there are still airplanes and yachts.

5) When you nail a hole into someone's soul, remember that even with apologies, you will still leave a hole that will continue to grow and torment its owner. Do not cause pain to those who love you.

6) Never make excuses. Neither to those who are deeply interested in you, nor, even more so, to those who are not thrilled by you. Those who are not thrilled will never believe you anyway, and those who are in love with you will come up with excuses for you themselves.

7) If gossip clouds your reputation, it means you are significant. Remember: talentless people are never discussed or envied. Talented people are envied and talked about.

8) If you can't make a woman happy, don't get in the way of someone else who can.

9) Learn to forgive, for it is a quality reserved for the strong. The weak never let go.

10) Fight, because a woman chooses the strong, brave, and persistent, not those who give up and run away at the first conflict.

11) Never argue with idiots. You will stoop to their level, where they will overwhelm you with their knowledge.

12) Always remember that people become close gradually, while strangers come instantly.

13) Learn to rejoice without alcohol, dream without drugs, communicate without the internet, and be calm without cigarettes.

14) Choose a job you love, and you won't have to work a single day in your life.

15) Don't rely too heavily on anyone in this world, because even your own shadow leaves you when you are in darkness.

16) Don't regret your mistakes, as without experiencing them, you will never learn how to do things right.

17) If you want to have what you have never had, you have to do what you have never done.

18) When you lose, simply smile. The winner loses the taste of victory.

19) You must have a dream. Absolutely! So that you can wake up in the mornings.

20) If a bear ties you to a tree while saying something in Latin, go to sleep. In any unclear situation, go to sleep.

21) If you're not satisfied with the place you're in, change it. After all, you're not a tree!

22) No one can disappoint you if you don't depend on anyone.

23) If you are wealthy, don't think about it. If you are poor, don't take your poverty seriously. If you are capable of living in the world, remembering that the world is just a stage, you will be free, untouched by suffering. Suffering is the result of a serious approach to life; bliss is the result of play. Take life as a game and enjoy it!

24) Do what you want. Do what you believe is right. You will still be judged for your entire life.

25) Never speak ill of yourself. There will always be those who will do it for you.
