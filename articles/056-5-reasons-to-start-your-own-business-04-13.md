# 5 reasons to start your own business

![5 reasons to start your own business](../images/056-5-reasons-to-start-your-own-business-04-13.jpg)

**1. New horizons.**

The first reason to start your own business is that your own business will open new horizons for you and allow you to achieve great success in all aspects of life. When you work for hire, the company management takes credit for all your successes, and many people eventually stop developing professionally, preferring to do only the minimum that their bosses require of them. And this, in turn, negatively affects not only earnings and career growth, but also the development of a person as an individual. Thus, we get a vicious circle, which can only be broken by starting to work for ourselves. This will give new strength and motivation for hard work and development, help you become an proactive and intelligently risk-taking person, and ultimately lead to the expected success.

**2. Freedom of action.**

Running your own business gives people greater freedom of action. Having started your own business, you will work only for yourself - you no longer need to be afraid of being late for work, asking for time off from the clinic, or listening to moral lectures from your boss. You can set your own daily routine. Independence from an employer is what most people create their own business for.

**3. Strengthening character.**

Thirdly, running your own business will give you capital that will stay with you for life - this is your character. Having started your own business, you will have to take full responsibility for its well-being. You will become more proactive and decisive, knowing that there is no boss over you and you are the very person who is responsible for everything. On the other hand, you will own not only the responsibility, but also all the success and profits of your enterprise.

**4. Implementation of your ideas.**

Fourthly, if you have ideas and a desire to implement them, then your own business will help you successfully bring them to life and extract maximum profit from them. Many modern successful corporations began as small companies created by enthusiasts - people who wanted to achieve success and purposefully pursued their dreams.

**5. A chance to change the world.**

Owning your own business will allow you to change the world. When Steve Jobs invited Pepsi executive John Scali to head Apple, he told him: “Do you want to spend your life selling sweet water or get a chance to change the world?” If you have any ideas about how people should live, if you want to bring something new into the world, then the best way to do this is to stand at the helm of your own company.
