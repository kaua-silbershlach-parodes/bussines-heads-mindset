# Interesting facts about business

![Interesting facts about business](../images/073-Interesting-facts-about-business-04-27.jpg)

1. According to statistics, 90% of restaurants close in the first year after opening.

2. Tuesday is the most productive day of the work week.

3. About 33 new products appear in the world every day, 13 of which are toys.

4. Every day about 65 people become millionaires.

5. Coffee is the second most traded commodity in the world after gasoline.

6. About 80% of all toys in the world were made in China.

7. The main income of the McDonald's company comes from drinks, regardless of the country of location.

8. Children's products in stores are placed as low as possible to the floor so that children can notice them.

9. About 20% of millionaires have never attended university.

10. American Airlines managed to save about 40 thousand dollars by removing just one olive from the salads served to business class passengers.

11. Pleasant smells in a store are in most cases artificial; they are created by air flavorings designed to entice the buyer to make a purchase. This method is especially effective for hungry visitors.

12. Fast food establishments often play fast music, because with such music, customers eat their food faster, which means they free up seats for new customers faster.

13. Google founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin wanted to sell Google to Yahoo for $1 billion in 1998, but Yahoo then refused such a purchase. 7 years later, in 2005, Google was already worth $80 billion, with annual profits of $1.5 billion.

14. The annual turnover of the advertising services market is half a trillion dollars. For comparison, the annual tobacco turnover in the world is about 400 billion dollars, which is 100 billion less.
