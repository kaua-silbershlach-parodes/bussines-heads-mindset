# What not to waste time on

![What not to waste time on](../images/065-What-not-to-waste-time-on-04-14.jpg)

1. Money for the sake of money. With them you can buy something that will allow you to enjoy life, surprise your family, travel... But when all your energy and time are devoted to making money, you lose the taste of life.

2. Unhappy relationships. There are a thousand reasons to stay in a relationship that brings nothing but resentment and dissatisfaction. But is it worth it?

3. Violence. Distance yourself from people who physically or psychologically abuse you. Also, analyze whether you are committing violence against yourself: unloved work, sacrificing your interests for the sake of loved ones, toxic communication...

4. Envy. This feeling is not as simple as anger or joy - envy is not so easy to control. This is precisely why she is dangerous. But there are antidotes for it, so work with this problem.

5. Nitpicking and petty quarrels. Sometimes you are so tired that any little thing can make you angry. Remember at these moments that a quarrel will take even more energy from you, and you are unlikely to benefit from it.

6. Existence "on draft". Nobody knows if tomorrow will come. Don't wait for a special moment to finally get a dog, see the ocean, or change careers. The future you will definitely not be happy that you are procrastinating today.

7. Lies and excuses. Trying to maintain your brand with lies, omissions and excuses, you yourself may not notice how you have lost yourself. Sometimes it's better to tell the inconvenient truth but stay true to yourself.
