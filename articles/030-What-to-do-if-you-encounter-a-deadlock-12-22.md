# What to do if you encounter a deadlock

![What to do if you encounter a deadlock](../images/030-What-to-do-if-you-encounter-a-deadlock-12-22.jpg)

Regardless of what you are doing - developing a skill, building a business, sooner or later your progress will slow down. And no matter how hard you try, the result will not come. It may even seem like you are moving backwards. Here's what you need to do in such a situation.

**1. Understand that it's a good thing.**

In nature, healthy progress happens over time. For example, when it gets cold, trees and animals go into hibernation, waiting for warmer weather. The same goes for human achievements. At different times, you will move forward at a great speed, and sometimes you will crawl like a snail. The fact that you have reached a plateau means that your success is natural and sustainable, and it is not an artificial bubble that will soon burst.

**2. Believe in yourself.**

Even if it seems like you are stuck, the mere fact that you have come this far proves that you can go further. Get inspired and find the source of your motivation, the goal that drives you in the right direction. Understand why you are pursuing this goal, why it is worth it, even when it seems like your efforts are not being rewarded.

**3. Continue to focus on the main current tasks.**

Thomas Edison said that genius is 1% inspiration and 99% perspiration. Sweating means waking up every day and practicing the core skills and tasks. This is what athletes and entrepreneurs do. It is the daily attention to details that ultimately pays off.

**4. Acknowledge your deadlock.**

It may seem like you have fallen into a pit - you are practicing the fundamentals, but it seems like everything is getting worse. Fewer successful deals, wider winnings for competitors you easily defeated before. It is during these moments that many people give up: it seems absolutely hopeless. But don't give up. Yes, it is worth even noting this moment, because failures are a clear signal that your progress on the plateau is coming to an end. And you are close to the next step, to...

**5. Experience a breakthrough.**

If, when in a deadlock, you do not lose your determination, you will make an important discovery, grasp an idea that will allow you to reach a new level. As long as you continue to hope, dream, as long as you are focused on your craft, a breakthrough is inevitable. It will happen.
