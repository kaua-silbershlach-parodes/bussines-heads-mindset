# Failure is the key to success

![Failure is the key to success](../images/069-Failure-is-the-key-to-success-04-14.jpg)

Let's talk about failures. Most people (or maybe there are none here), including me, are accustomed to having a negative attitude towards failure. They are seen as a failure, proof that your plan was not successful or your ideas are not good enough.

But the truth is that failure happens to everyone. One of the things that sets successful people apart is a true understanding of the power of failure. To achieve success, we must learn from mistakes and missteps along the way, and not give up and despair.

To change your attitude towards failures, the following tips will help you and me.

If you failed, then you tried.

The best way to measure your progress in any area is to count the number of failures you have had. If you haven't failed, chances are you haven't tried very hard. Failure is the blacksmith's hammer that forges the sword of your success. If you really want to succeed at something, you have to fail at least a couple of times.

If you look at all the great men and women in history, you will notice that they have one thing in common. They failed, and they failed often. Remember Thomas Edison. How many times did he make a mistake when choosing the filament material for his light bulb? There are different assumptions, but they are all within the “damn many” range.
