# Marketing for Small Businesses. 10 recommendations for success

![Marketing for Small Businesses. 10 recommendations for success](../images/019-Marketing-for-Small-Businesses-10-recommendations-for-success-12-14.jpg)

**1) Narrow down your market**

Develop a profile of the ideal customer: their appearance, thoughts, evaluations, where can you find them? Do not target those who do not fit the definition of an ideal customer.

**2) Differentiate yourself**

Get rid of all the information about your product. Focus on the main idea and work with it. Make sure this idea sets you apart from competitors.

**3) Strategic thinking**

Combine steps 1 and 2 and develop your strategy. Capture the minds of consumers with one or two words. Free words. Of course, by consumer, we mean your ideal customer.

**4) Create educational materials**

Your company is informational. Your website, marketing materials, and others - all of these are educational materials, informational, not just advertising brochures.

**5) Emotions and visual style**

Cleverly use visual styles to evoke an emotional response from your ideal client. They should match their preferences. Every element of promotion with a visual component must be carefully thought out.

**6) Obtain potential clients from different sources**

People learn in different ways. Try to attract clients from different situations. This will allow you to gain experience and better understand the most effective methods of attracting potential clients.

**7) Build a logical path to purchasing a product**

Providing information leads to a logical path - the purchase of a product. Create a conversion system that will convince them that you have what they need. Do this before, during, and after the purchase of the product.

**8) Always measure what matters**

Certain things always matter. Often, the secret to business success lies in intangible assets and their measurement. This often leads to profit.

**9) Automate**

Automate marketing processes. Utilize online communities, create an information base, automate the key elements of your information business. Allow people to spread information about you.

**10) Plan**

Constantly plan your marketing activities. Create plans for the day, week, month, quarter, and even year. Make sure your marketing investments align with your plan.
