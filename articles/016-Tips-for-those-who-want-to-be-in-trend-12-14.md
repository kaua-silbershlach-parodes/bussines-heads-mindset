# Tips for those who want to be in trend

![Tips for those who want to be in trend](../images/016-Tips-for-those-who-want-to-be-in-trend-12-14.jpg)

The world is becoming more diverse, and sometimes you can't catch up with everything that happens in it. We tell you what current trends exist and what you need to do to stay informed.

**Healthy lifestyle.**

Being healthy is not just fashionable, but a widely accepted practice. People strive for proper nutrition, regular exercise, and giving up unhealthy food. Start slowly: do exercises at work and leave on time, walk more, and try to consume light food.

**Digital minimalism.**

In the era of information overload, it is important to not only control what goes into our stomachs but also into our minds. From the common desire for a healthy lifestyle, another trend has emerged - digital minimalism. Regularly allocate time without gadgets that distract from the main thing, clean up your subscriptions in social networks, and limit the time spent on them.

**Consumer consciousness.**

"Less is more" - the new slogan for all of humanity. People are tired of constant promotions and new collections, realizing that things do not make them happier (except, perhaps, for temporary effect). Carefully consider your expenses and keep only the essentials.

**Eco-friendliness.**

Unrestrained consumption clutters not only our apartment but also the surrounding nature. This understanding has led to a trend of eco-friendliness - humanity has become important to preserve the environment. To take steps in this direction, do not buy plastic bags in supermarkets and carry a reusable coffee cup with you - this way you will already do a lot for our planet.

**Investing.**

Investing in one's future is another necessity of the modern person. This modern philosophy covers educational, spiritual, and financial spheres. So sign up for programming courses, try meditation, and engage in investing. Don't be afraid of the last one - it's much easier than it seems. You can open an Individual Investment Account (IIA) at any bank convenient for you, even through an app. Entrust the management of your assets to professionals. And you will be able to receive income and even return 13% of your income back to yourself.
