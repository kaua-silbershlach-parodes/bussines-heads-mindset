# 20 signs that you can become a successful entrepreneur

![20 signs that you can become a successful entrepreneur](../images/013-20-signs-that-you-can-become-a-successful-entrepreneur-12-14.jpg)

Entrepreneurs are a special group of people who not only possess a distinctive way of thinking but also take unique actions. Generating ideas that lie at the intersection of madness and genius, these individuals rely on unparalleled personal traits, habits, and intellectual abilities. But don't think that being a talented inventor with a concept for replacing gasoline with alternative fuel makes you capable of becoming an entrepreneur.

If you have ever wondered if you belong to the aforementioned group of people, compare the traits of your personality and behavior with the items on the following list. You may not possess all the characteristics, but having at least some of them is an indicator of having an entrepreneurial streak.

**1. Your relatives have never worked for someone else**

Your parents were engaged in their own business. However, this statement does not apply to all entrepreneurs: some people from such families were wage workers.

**2. You can't stand the current situation**

You always question why people do what they do. You strive to improve everything within your capabilities and are willing to make efforts for that.

**3. You believe in yourself**

Have you ever seen an entrepreneur who was pessimistic about themselves? Indeed, if you don't believe in yourself, how will others believe in you? Most entrepreneurs evaluate themselves only positively.

**4. You are a passionate person**

During the course of doing business, there may be a situation where you spend a lot of time and energy but don't make money. In such a situation, only your passion will allow you to keep going.

**5. You don't take no for an answer**

An entrepreneur never gives up. Never.

**6. You are good at building business relationships with strangers**

People are generally attracted to you because of your charm. Mostly because of your passion.

**7. You spend more time with your business partner than with your life partner**

**8. You dropped out of school like Bill Gates, Steve Jobs, and Mark Zuckerberg**

**9. Every morning, you go to your office — from the bedroom to the living room**

**10. You have always been considered a failure and have probably been fired several times**

Don't worry, you are not alone. It doesn't reflect poorly on you. Perhaps you are just not meant for employment.

**11. You are always against authority**

That's why you couldn't stay in one job for long.

**12. You have job security**

You understand that you are not at risk of unemployment as long as you control the situation and don't rely on a boss who can destroy your career at any moment.

**13. Most of your clothing consists of t-shirts**

And, let's be honest, they are not from the most expensive brands. Perhaps they have inscriptions related to your interests or your company logo.

**14. You are a persistent person**

You always know that you can do something better.

**15. You check GitHub every morning**

**16. You accept payment in tickets to matches, shoes, or other items that you love**

After all, there is something better than money, right?

**17. Your ideal day off is a work day without distractions**

**18. You can't find a job**

This is not a bad thing. Life skills are more valuable than corporate culture.

**19. You work more than 60 hours a week**

While earning more than in school.

**20. You want to be both the leader and the executor in your company**

Generally, you prefer to control all the processes related to the functioning of your company.
