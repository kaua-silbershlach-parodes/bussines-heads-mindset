# The thinking of a successful person

![The thinking of a successful person](../images/008-The-thinking-of-a-successful-person-12-14.jpg)

Every person is born for happiness. Absolutely everyone. And only our own attitude towards life, our thoughts, affect whether we feel happy and believe in our ability to achieve success. It is our thinking that determines where and how we are moving.

Most people believe that successful people always have luck on their side, that they have a good start - inheritance, wealthy parents, in a word, a good initial capital. Certainly, there are such cases. But far from all people who have achieved significant success in life started with a substantial sum of money. Among successful people, there are many who have experienced numerous failures and even bankruptcy. This only confirms that it is not about the starting capital. The main secret of any successful person is their thinking, which fundamentally differs from the thinking of losers.

1. Successful people are positive. They always enjoy life, despite all obstacles. They can find the good in almost everything. It is always pleasant to communicate with them because they are cheerful and friendly. Such people are literally infectious with their positivity.

2. Successful people believe in themselves and in their success. They never tell themselves "I won't succeed at anything," because they are already predisposed to exert maximum efforts to make everything work out. But even if something doesn't work out, they, unlike losers, ask themselves "How and what can I do in this situation?" and look for ways to achieve positive results.

3. Successful people are not afraid of mistakes because they understand that only those who do nothing make no mistakes. Mistakes provide experience, and experience is the most valuable acquisition.

4. Successful people never seek excuses for their inaction. They understand perfectly well that excuses are of no interest to anyone. Only results matter.

5. Successful people are always honest with themselves. If they lack the knowledge or experience to solve a particular task, they are capable of admitting it and are not afraid to seek help. If they are overwhelmed by fear, they find the strength within themselves to overcome it and act further.

6. Successful people are persistent in achieving their goals, perceiving any difficulties not as problems but as tasks that need to be solved.

7. Successful people are not afraid of responsibility. They make decisions independently, even if these decisions contradict the opinions of others.

8. Successful people know that the best investment is an investment in themselves, in their development, education, and health. Meanwhile, losers prefer to buy another trinket instead of a useful book.

9. Successful people do not fixate on what has become outdated (work, business, relationships, etc.). They do not try to ride a dead horse in the hope of reviving it.

10. Successful people are always IN favor of mutually beneficial partnership. They understand that building relationships based on mutual benefit contributes to long-term cooperation.

11. And finally, successful people are always active. They prefer to take action and make full use of the opportunities available to them.

As you can see, everything depends on the attitude towards a particular situation, towards life itself. Think positively. Smile at life – and life will definitely smile back at you.
