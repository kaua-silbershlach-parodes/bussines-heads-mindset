# 7 Ways to Develop Perseverance

![7 Ways to Develop Perseverance](../images/048-7-Ways-to-Develop-Perseverance-01-15.jpg)

**1. Study the lives of successful people.**

Understanding that perseverance has helped many achieve success will inspire you to develop this quality in yourself. Find and read biographies of successful people. While most only focus on their achievements, focus your attention on their perseverance that allowed them to achieve their goals.

**2. Don't expect it to be easy, prepare for the hard way.**

One of the main reasons people give up is due to incorrect expectations. They expect it to be easy and are very surprised when in reality it turns out the opposite. Their enthusiasm quickly fades and they become discouraged.

Start your path to success with the right expectations. Remember that success comes at a price. Expect the journey to be difficult - this will help you mentally prepare for the reality.

**3. Don't underestimate the amount of time required.**

Just as you should not underestimate the difficulty of the road ahead, you should not underestimate its length. Of course, everyone wants to achieve success instantly, but, unfortunately, this is impossible. The path to success is a marathon, not a sprint, and you must be prepared for it.

**4. Have strong motivation.**

To maintain your persistence and persistence, you need a strong source of motivation. In order to not give up and keep trying to succeed again and again, you need a strong reason. It usually follows from your goal. You must be clear about why you want to achieve your goal. Your reason must be stronger than the obstacles you encounter along the way.

**5. Treat failures correctly.**

Since failures are inevitable on the path to success, you must know how to handle them. This will help you persevere. When you don't know how to handle setbacks and they take you by surprise, your mental energy quickly runs out.

**6. Find partners and like-minded people.**

It will be easier to achieve success if you are surrounded by partners and like-minded people who support you. They will motivate and support you in difficult moments of your life. Also, by sharing their experience with you, they will help you avoid many mistakes.

**7. Reduce your stress.**

To maintain your persistence, you must reduce the drain on your mental energy. Control your tension. If you don't do this, you won't be able to put in your best efforts to achieve your goals as you will lose a lot of energy.
