# 10 tips that will change your life

![10 tips that will change your life](../images/081-10-tips-that-will-change-your-life-04-27.jpg)

1. Set goals, but don't get attached to them.

2. Plan your day.

3. Surround yourself with worthy people who will contribute to your realization.

4. Be true to your dream.

5. Work while enjoying.

6. Learn from a professional mentor who will help accelerate your professional growth.

7. Remember: no matter how good you do, someone will always be dissatisfied. It's unavoidable.

8. Small daily improvements lead to massive success.

9. Greet people first and smile at them. Only a strong and successful person can allow himself to be the first to show goodwill.

10. It's never too late to start a new business, introduce a new habit and take up a new hobby. Keep looking for things that will broaden your horizons.
