# 10 tips for self-improvement

![10 tips for self-improvement](../images/074-10-tips-for-self-improvement-04-27.jpg)

**1. Meet the day wisely.**

Get up earlier, do exercises, have breakfast. The earlier you get up, the more things you will have time to do during the day.

**2. Make a schedule and stick to it.**

Try to follow the schedule you created. This will allow you to solve more problems each day. Keep a notepad where you can write down current tasks. And at the end of the day, take stock of what was done and what could have been done better.

**3. Don't forget about rest.**

The best rest is a change of activity. Therefore, try to do varied work and relax during the day. After all, quality rest gives you even more strength to work and move forward.

**4. Be generous.**

Try to be kind and generous with other people. A smile, good advice, a friendly word - everything that you give into this world, you receive in multiplied quantities.

**5. Accept what you cannot change.**

There are things that cannot be changed. Don't waste your energy trying to make the world around you fit your needs. Learn to accept what you cannot change.

**6. Learn new languages.**

Learning foreign languages is one of the best ways to improve your understanding of your native language. In the process of studying, you give your brain a task, which ultimately allows it to work even better.

**7. Eliminate routine.**

Try to be a little more well-rounded person. Don't let your life become ordinary and boring. Try new things, gain new experience and new knowledge, expand your horizons!

**8. Face your fears.**

The more fears you have, the more difficult it becomes to live life to the fullest. It doesn't matter what you're afraid of, what matters is how you deal with your fears. Overcome your fears day by day, meter by meter expanding your comfort zone.

**9. Live in the present.**

It is not the goal that is important, but the journey to the goal. Don't forget about the present moment! Life doesn’t stand still, and it would be unreasonable to deprive yourself of the joy of living here and now for the sake of a momentary jubilation at the moment of achieving your goal.

**10. Stop procrastinating.**

Take control of your life! Think about what you want to achieve. And right now, decide to plan your work, rest and entertainment. If you intended to work today, but your mood is at zero and you don’t raise your hands, still force yourself to work. Anything is better than mindlessly sitting on the couch and watching TV.
