# Increased productivity. 7 tips.

![Increased productivity. 7 tips.](../images/075-Increased-productivity-7-tips-04-27.jpg)

1. Plan your schedule. Plan everything to take control of your everyday life. Including time for joy and relaxation, of course.

2. Fill your calendar to the brim. Gaps in your schedule are time that you will waste 90% of the time.

3. Recharge. Most useful actions take away your energy, but there are also those that do the exact opposite - they charge and motivate. Include such responsibilities in your daily schedule.

4. Prioritize tasks. We often tend to do things that are not the most important on the agenda. As a result, by ignoring what is important, you feel depressed in the end. Do what you want most first.

5. Do the hardest thing first. This sets a positive mood for the whole day: you will always feel that there is little left. Do the hard stuff in the first 2 hours of the day.

6. Get up early. It doesn't have to be 4 or 5 a.m. wake up an hour earlier than your usual wake-up time. This is +1 hour for a productive routine. It will be nice if you spend it on solving complex problems.

7. Smile often and keep your back straight. Your movements generate emotions - check. A straight back, for example, makes you feel more confident. This means that solving problems will be easier. Experiment.
