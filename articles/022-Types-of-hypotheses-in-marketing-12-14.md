# Types of hypotheses in marketing

![Types of hypotheses in marketing](../images/022-Types-of-hypotheses-in-marketing-12-14.jpg)

The main business process of marketing is constant hypothesis generation and testing. And the sooner you do it, the faster you find the right solution.

How many hypotheses are currently being worked on within your marketing department, within your sales department? Usually one, maximum two.

Usually, the format of presenting advertising messages is most often tested. However, this is not enough to achieve success.

In practice, your task is to continuously test hypotheses:

- in the context of your product;

- your competitive strategy;

- your target market;

- target segments of your market;

- geography of your operations;

- customer acquisition logic;

Testing hypotheses is an important process that should continue uninterrupted.

Do not be afraid to think outside the box. Success is usually hidden behind the door ;)
