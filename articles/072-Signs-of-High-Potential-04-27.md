# Signs of High Potential

![Signs of High Potential](../images/072-Signs-of-High-Potential-04-27.jpg)

**1.Integrity.**

Conscientious people commit to plans and carry them out to the letter. Such people know how to overcome their impulses and evaluate the wisdom of their decisions in the long term. Conscientiousness is considered one of the most important predictors of life outcomes.

**2. Adaptability.**

Everyone faces challenges, but people who are adaptable cope better under pressure without allowing it to negatively influence their behavior and decision-making.

**3. Preparedness for uncertainty.**

People with a high tolerance for uncertainty can explore many more points of view before coming to a decision, which means they are less dogmatic and more flexible in their opinions.

**4. Curiosity.**

Curiosity has previously been somewhat ignored by psychologists. However, recent research shows that a keen interest in new ideas has many benefits at work: it can mean you're more creative and flexible in using different techniques, helps you learn more easily, increases overall job satisfaction and protects against burnout.

**5. Risk appetite.**

Do you prefer to avoid potential unpleasant confrontations? Or will you move on, knowing that short-term discomfort will resolve the situation and bring long-term benefits? It's no surprise that the ability to handle difficult situations is critical to management positions.

**6. Competitiveness.**

There is a fine line between the desire for personal success and unhealthy envy of the success of others. Competitiveness at its best can be a powerful motivation that will force you not to stop at what you have achieved. But in the worst case scenario, it can lead to the destruction of the team.
