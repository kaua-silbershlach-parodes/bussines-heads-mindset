# Rules for independent development to achieve goals

![Rules for independent development to achieve goals](../images/063-Rules-for-independent-development-to-achieve-goals-04-14.jpg)

1. Clear your head of unnecessary debris. Bad habits, wasting time, stress, any stupid troubles and problems. Take them and forget about them. For new achievements you need a bright head.

2. Educate yourself. Read books about business and those that you like. Visit all kinds of exhibitions, strange crazy places, travel, take up new sports, be comprehensively developed - this will help you a lot in business.

3. Develop the character of a leader, logic, business IQ, ability to communicate with people, develop gestures and facial expressions, manipulation, public speaking skills... Get involved in everything.

4. Businessmen see right through people. They can leave a crowd in amazement. They know human psychology better than any psychologist. Of course, this does not apply to all businessmen, I’m talking about those who have achieved a lot. And all this needs to be developed over the years. Work it up.

5. Love money. Once they love money, they will love you. We noticed that the poor say that they have no money and generally try to avoid this topic, while the rich like to talk about money, that they have a lot of it, and know how to do even more.
