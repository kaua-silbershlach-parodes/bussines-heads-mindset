# How to save money while running a small business?

![How to save money while running a small business?](../images/057-How-to-save-money-while-running-a-small-business-04-13.jpg)

What are the biggest expenses in your business? While some of them (rent, utilities and insurance) are natural, others hinder business growth. Maybe you are struggling with late payments from large clients? Or did you hire unreasonably overpaid employees? Let's discuss ways to reduce the hidden costs of doing business...

**1) Saving plan.**

The main principle of minimizing expenses is to write a financial plan and stick to it. To learn how to do this, you can attend courses at local business groups, listen to advice, get support and the opportunity to share an effective strategy.

If we consider savings in terms of the price of the product, the main thing is not to overdo it. After all, most customers want good value, not just the lowest price. In some cases, it makes sense to increase the price of one or two products and observe the results.

Many entrepreneurs start their business from home. This is a good way to keep costs to a minimum. You may have to notify local authorities about your business. It should also be taken into account that this approach will increase the cost of paying for utilities.

**2) Low-cost incentives for talented employees.**

Hiring permanent staff is expensive for a small business; it all depends on the skills of the individual. To find top talent for your company, you must compete with large organizations that offer incentives such as additional health insurance or other benefits.

The opportunity for career advancement is a real privilege for job seekers. Often employees are more concerned about this than additional bonuses. It is important to maintain the motivation and spirit of a particular employee.

If your business is able to offer shares to its employees, this can be a good incentive for them to strive for self-improvement.

**3) Dealing with late payments.**

When a client owes you a debt, the question always arises: to maintain a good relationship or demand payment of the debt? But several such late payments can lead to an unstable financial situation for the enterprise. In this case, when concluding a contract, it is necessary to stipulate in writing possible sanctions for late payment. One of them is the calculation of interest on the amount of the debt incurred.

**4) Use of outside expert assistance.**

As a small business, with a limited number of employees, and without all the skills needed to grow your company, you can sometimes turn to outside help in areas such as marketing or business consulting. Of course, you need to weigh the costs against the potential benefits to your business.

Modern marketing (for example, advertising on social networks) is more effective than traditional marketing (mailings, advertising in the press, etc.).

Business consulting can help you develop a new business plan. But it's not cheap. An alternative option could be a mentor. As a rule, such people do their work at a lower cost to you than professional business consultants.

The list of suggested tips is, of course, not exhaustive. But by following the basic rules of running a profitable business, your competitiveness in the modern market will increase significantly.
