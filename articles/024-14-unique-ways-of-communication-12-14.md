# 14 unique ways of communication

![14 unique ways of communication](../images/024-14-unique-ways-of-communication-12-14.jpg)

1. When you have to communicate with people, place a mirror behind your back. Most clients will demonstrate more polite behavior because no one wants to see themselves angry and annoyed.

2. If you expect sharp criticism from someone at a meeting, sit next to that person. In this case, most likely, they will become more gentle and will not be able to harm you as much as they could if they were at a distance from you.

3. Once you have expressed your position in an argument, do not say anything else, it can work against you. Wait and give your opponent time to think about your words and respond.

4. The sequence of arguments is important in a dispute. Start with a strong argument, then present a weaker one and finish with the most serious argument.

5. If someone tries to provoke you into anger, do not allow it and remain calm. This confuses your opponent and makes them follow your rules.

6. During conflict situations, try to avoid using "you" / "you". They often sound like an accusation and do not help much. Try saying "I think" or "it seems to me". No one will argue with your feelings. And this is a great start to finding a joint solution.

7. When meeting a new person, pay attention to the color of their eyes and smile. This way, you will hold their gaze for a few seconds longer and create a friendly impression.

8. Allow the child to make some decisions on their own, for example, the choice of shoes for a walk - neat or with dinosaurs. They will be glad that they can control the situation.

9. It is better to have the first date in a place where you can get more positive emotions. In the future, pleasant memories will be associated with you.

10. If you express sincere joy when meeting people, sooner or later they will start to react the same way. It may not happen immediately, but it always works.

11. During arguments, try to operate with proven facts, not vague opinions or rumors. This reduces the chance of getting into a ridiculous situation if the information turns out to be incorrect.

12. If your interlocutor's answer does not satisfy you or you feel that it is incomplete, do not ask again. Just look closely into the person's eyes. This will make them continue the conversation.

13. Learn to ask not only "closed" questions (which can be answered "yes" or "no"), but also "open" questions that require a detailed answer. A skillful combination of such questions in a dialogue will help you steer the conversation in the right direction.

14. After getting acquainted, address the person by name, even if they will leave in 5 minutes. It is better to say "Goodbye, Alexander!" instead of the usual "Goodbye!" - this creates a more trusting and friendly atmosphere.
