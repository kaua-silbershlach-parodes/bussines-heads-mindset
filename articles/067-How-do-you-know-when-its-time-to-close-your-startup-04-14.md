# How do you know when it’s time to close your startup?

![How do you know when it’s time to close your startup?](../images/067-How-do-you-know-when-its-time-to-close-your-startup-04-14.jpg)

It happens that a startup started working, demonstrated its viability, but never reached that scale. Which is what its creators were counting on. Sometimes this is a reason to adjust the business model. But it also happens that the best thing to do is close the project.

If you're wondering whether your business can survive another year or whether it's better to cut it short and start working on a new project, ask yourself the following questions.

1. Can it be sold?

Look at all your options before closing your business. If the problem is finances, think about it: is there a large company that will want to acquire your business in order to get your talent? Can you sell securities to raise investment capital?

2. Does your business partner share your point of view?

Your partners may be more or less optimistic about the company's prospects. Perhaps it makes sense to agree on a deadline until which the project will live. If the project does not show better results up to this point, it makes sense to close it.

3. Can I try again?

If, while working on a project, you have formed a team of specialists, but the project itself has actually become obsolete, perhaps it makes sense to do something else. Perhaps your next project will be much more successful than the previous one.
