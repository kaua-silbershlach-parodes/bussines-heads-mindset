# The best way to "pump up" yourself (become smarter, more creative, and more successful) is to read books. Which ones?

![The best way to "pump up" yourself (become smarter, more creative, and more successful) is to read books. Which ones?](../images/003-The-best-way-to-pump-up-yourself-become-smarter-more-creative-and-more-successful-is-to-read-books-Which-ones-12-14.jpg)

**Start. Punch Fear In The Face, Escape Average and Do Work That Matters**

**Author: John Acuff**

A book about how to color your gray life in bright tones. Being like everyone else is the easiest thing to do. Being an unconventional person is a deed worthy of recognition. No matter how old you are, no matter what your social privileges are, it all comes down to one simple truth: you have to start. After reading this book, you will truly want to start taking action, start changing yourself and your life. And when better to start a new life than in the New Year?

**This Year I Will… How to Finally Change a Habit, Keep Resolution, or Make a Dream Come True**

**Author: M. J. Ryan**

A book about how to change habits, keep promises, or fulfill long-held dreams. Usually, such life promises are made during the New Year, and after the holidays, they are successfully "forgotten". The book collects the author's knowledge in the fields of psychology, neurophysiology, and philosophy, which have translated into practical recommendations on how to fulfill promises made under the chimes.

**Eat That Frog! 21 Great Ways to Stop Procrastinating and Get More Done in Less Time**

**Author: Brian Tracy**

A book about how to properly manage your time and solve difficult tasks by stepping out of your comfort zone. It contains 21 practical pieces of advice that can help you become as successful as the author. Brian Tracy is a millionaire who dropped out of school in his youth and started his path from scratch. This book is a compact and useful collection of Brian Tracy's personal effectiveness secrets. It has been translated into 40 languages and has a circulation of over 1,200,000 copies.

**The Personal MBA. Master the Art of Business**

**Author: Josh Kaufman**

A book about self-education and self-belief. They are more important than academic degrees. The idea of the book is that there is no need to spend money on business school education. It is cheaper and more effective to engage in self-education, especially in the age of information technology. The book contains dozens of statements that teach a completely new business mindset. Josh Kaufman's advice is truly inspiring. The book can replace an MBA on many levels.

**The Willpower Instinct**

**Author: Kelly McGonigal**

A book about becoming mentally strong, resisting temptations, and stopping procrastination. The book exudes scientific rigor. All the advice is backed by numerous psychological and medical studies. That is why the book is useful not only for those in need of strengthening their willpower, but also for curious people who will find plenty of interesting insights.
