# How to develop business thinking

![How to develop business thinking](../images/009-How-to-develop-business-thinking-12-14.jpg)

Business thinking is a tool for attracting clients. The better it is developed in an entrepreneur, the more clients he can attract. This helps the entrepreneur see many opportunities for making money. Its development is beneficial for both beginners and experienced businessmen who strive for constant growth in sales and profits.

Business thinking as a success factor

Where an ordinary person sees obstacles and problems, a businessman sees an opportunity to increase his capital, create new sources of growth. The development of business thinking is necessary for everyone who is engaged in or intends to engage in business. It is not a given at birth, so it needs to be developed. The better it is developed, the more success can be achieved.

If an entrepreneur has not developed business thinking, he faces either the "specialist syndrome" or the "life syndrome". The specialist syndrome is when a businessman does business while remaining essentially a specialist in a particular field. The life syndrome is when a businessman conducts business based on his subjective worldview.

The main skill of a businessman is attracting clients. A person with developed business thinking does this easily and naturally. If a person has not developed it, then he is not a businessman, but simply a specialist in a specific field and relies on someone to bring him clients or they will find him on their own. The specialist believes that his task is to do the work.

A specialist cannot attract clients, he can only serve them. He experiences the "specialist syndrome," which in 99% of cases leads to bankruptcy. Specialists believe that there are many ready-made clients who will find them on their own. While they think so, true businessmen actively attract clients and leave specialists without work.

An ordinary person is also incapable of attracting clients. He can only communicate on a human level. Accordingly, in cases where it is necessary to behave like a businessman and earn money, he behaves like an ordinary person. He believes that it is enough to just communicate with the client to interest him. At this time, true businessmen actively attract clients.

Business, in its essence, has a certain idea. In fact, business is simply the embodiment of this idea. Only someone in whom it is "implanted" and who has honed it well can implement it. The person who has honed the idea best is capable of creating the most effective business. Overcoming the "specialist syndrome" and the "life syndrome" involves the implementation of the idea and its deep honing.

Business thinking is developed through the implementation of the business idea and its honing using the business grammar methodology or the "Immersion in Business" training. However, it is not enough to just implement the business idea. It is also necessary to have a good understanding of the market, for which the idea of the market needs to be honed. The implementation of the market idea and its honing is done through the market grammar methodology.

The most advanced businessmen are those with the most developed business thinking. They see opportunities that are unnoticed by others, and this is what allows them to make money where others cannot. Anyone can become an advanced businessman after developing business thinking. Anyone who makes an effort can become an effective businessman.
