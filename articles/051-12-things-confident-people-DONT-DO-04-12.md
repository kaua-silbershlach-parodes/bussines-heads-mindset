# 12 things confident people DON'T DO

![12 things confident people DON'T DO](../images/051-12-things-confident-people-DONT-DO-04-12.jpg)

**1. They don’t make excuses.**

These people are not trying to avoid responsibility for their words and actions. They don't use excuses like "I just didn't have time." They admit their mistakes and try to correct them.

**2. They don’t refuse to do things that scare them.**

Don't let fear enslave you. They know that often the actions they are afraid to do are necessary steps towards achieving their goals.

**3. Don't stay too long in your comfort zone.**

They know that staying in their comfort zone is not productive. There is no room for progress in the comfort zone.

**4. Don't put things off until next week.**

Confident people understand that a good plan executed today is better than a great plan executed “someday.”

**5. Don't get hung up on the opinions of others.**

Don’t let yourself get bogged down by negative reviews from others. Of course, they care about the well-being of others, but at the same time they do not allow their opinions to negatively affect their own productivity.

**6. Don't judge people.**

Confident people do not feel the need to talk about others behind their backs or attack those whose opinions do not coincide with their own.

**7. Don't let a lack of resources stop them from achieving their goals.**

Confident people use the opportunities, even the smallest ones, that they have now, and do not waste time and energy complaining about the fact that they do not have all the necessary conditions for self-realization.

**8. Don't compare yourself to others.**

They do not compete with anyone other than themselves.

**9. They don’t try to please everyone.**

Confident people know that not all people get along. Therefore, they focus on the quality of the relationship rather than the quantity.

**10. Don’t run away from solving problems.**

Confident people understand that problems cannot be solved on their own. And if the problem is not solved now, it will most likely only grow over time.

**11. They are not stopped by failures.**

They know that only those who do nothing do not make mistakes, and that there is no progress without failures.

**12. They don’t wait for permission to take action.**

They do not wait for advice or a sign from above. They themselves are able to determine when to start.
