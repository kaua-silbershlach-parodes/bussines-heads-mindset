# How to convince a person: 10 simple tips

![How to convince a person: 10 simple tips](../images/059-How-to-convince-a-person-10-simple-tips-04-13.jpg)

Rules of persuasion: rhetoric, argumentation:

1. Never drive a person into a corner, be extremely polite and careful.

2. Use the above argument rotation carefully think through your speech.

3. Gain trust using practical knowledge. Tell what benefits and dividends your knowledge brings to you. Staff persuasion rules, for example, bring this position to the fore.

4. Be sure to agree on something with your opponent. This little trick will allow the interlocutor to feel respect for his beliefs.

5. Flatter, but not rudely. Crude flattery is repulsive. To gain trust and relax a person, this technique requires subtlety.

6. Use the above rule for the sequence of agreements according to Socrates.

7. Steer the conversation away from dangerous topics and “sharp corners” if they are not relevant to the subject of the conversation.

8. Consider every little detail. Facial expressions, gestures, verbal and non-verbal behavior - all this is of great importance. The rules of persuasion in advertising pay special attention to this proposition.

9. Remember that a person is looking for benefits. Reveal to him the most profitable, advantageous sides of your position and convey to him that his position remains simply his position for nothing - it does not bring (and will not bring!) the desired benefit, benefit, profit.

10. Even if your interlocutor is annoying, show him respect and attentiveness, but under no circumstances be sycophantic! An adequate person will always appreciate such a gesture, knowing that you do not agree with him. Perhaps in this way he will single you out in a dispute. The rules of persuasion in business communication especially highlight this position and necessarily focus attention on it.

Having mastered this information and using it in practice, you will see how effectively the given recommendations work. Don't be upset if success doesn't come right away. The main thing is to control your emotions and practice. The rules for persuading people involve hard work on oneself, mental and spiritual tension, strict control of behavior and rhetoric.
