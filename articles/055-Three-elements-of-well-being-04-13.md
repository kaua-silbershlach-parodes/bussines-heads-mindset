# Three elements of well-being

![Three elements of well-being](../images/055-Three-elements-of-well-being-04-13.jpg)

**1. Financial well-being.**

Money helps you be a happier person, especially when you don't have to think about money.

Money is what gives me an understanding of my value, as well as a means of realizing what is important to me.

⠀

**2️. Satisfying needs.**

If I, as a hedonist, have the opportunity to fully satisfy my needs: love, communication with loved ones, sleep, food, self-development, then I can do a lot.

⠀

**3️. Mental state.**

When there are no boundaries in your head and you can come up with and implement all your ideas! I control my emotions and have the energy to overcome difficulties. I do whatever comes to my mind, and it's great fun!
