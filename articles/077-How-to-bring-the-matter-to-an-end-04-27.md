# How to bring the matter to an end?

![How to bring the matter to an end?](../images/077-How-to-bring-the-matter-to-an-end-04-27.jpg)

It is not always possible to finish a job that has been started. There are many reasons: lack of time, overwork, and even a dog that asks for a walk.

Sometimes we are not able to finish a task because there is no person nearby who “motivates with a slap in the face.” Although you can do without it.

For these reasons, many will consider themselves not disciplined enough. They will think that there are gifted people who can do it, but they can’t.

The truth is that any person is capable of completing the task he takes on. You just need to follow these tips:

1. Abstract yourself from everything that interferes. Leave nearby only those things that contribute to your work.

2. It is important to anticipate possible obstacles and prepare for them in advance - try to look ahead and at least turn off notifications on your phone.

3. Take time to relax. It will save you from overload and reduce stress levels.

4. Break the work into parts. It is much easier to complete small tasks than one large one.

5. If your friends invited you to go for a walk, and you planned to work, don’t agree. Say you're busy and move on.

6. Don't rush to achieve your goal - slow down. You will immediately begin to work more meaningfully and purposefully - reducing the chances of making mistakes. There will be time to think about next steps.

The next time you want to give up halfway, remember these points and continue moving towards your goal.
