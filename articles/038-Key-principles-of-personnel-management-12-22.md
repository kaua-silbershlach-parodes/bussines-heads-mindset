# Key principles of personnel management

![Key principles of personnel management](../images/038-Key-principles-of-personnel-management-12-22.jpg)

Forget what you learned from specialized literature on personnel management. In fact, there is only one way to ensure that each of your subordinates will be an outstanding employee.

Not all books on personnel management touch on this topic. Most of them try to teach you how to manage "people".

Simply managing "people" is impossible. Due to the individuality of each person, a method that works for one may be ineffective for another.

Certain employees succeed when they are publicly praised, while others thrive when they are specifically distinguished. Some depend on salary, while others value challenging and engaging projects. Some require a mentor for constant support, while advice annoys others.

The key idea is to manage personnel according to their own desires, rather than based on your skills or convenience.

The only way to find out is to ask. In your first (or next) meeting with them, ask the following questions:

What can I do to make it even more convenient for you to work?

What will help you become more effective?

What aspects of management irritate you?

Listen carefully to their answers and adapt your methods to each employee.

By the way, some employees won't expect you to ask them these questions - they may directly tell you what would be better and more convenient for them at work. And when that happens - don't forget to take note of it!
