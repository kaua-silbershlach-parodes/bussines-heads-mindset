# 4 main questions about finances that you need to know the answers to

![4 main questions about finances that you need to know the answers to](../images/027-4-main-questions-about-finances-that-you-need-to-know-the-answers-to-12-14.jpg)

1. What is the amount of your debt?

To quickly deal with debts, it is necessary to know exactly how much you owe. It is also important to understand how much time it will take to repay them. Without this, it is impossible to develop a clear repayment plan, which is necessary.

Calculate how many years and months it will take to repay the debts, and when you can start increasing the payments. If you are in a long-term relationship, it is important to know how much debt your partner has, as this also affects you.

2. How much savings do you have?

This will help you understand how much you spend and how much you save, as well as plan your emergency fund.

If you have accumulated a sufficient amount for several months, consider transferring it to retirement savings, investments, or other major goals. This is especially important to know if you plan budgeting together with your spouse.

Having a clear understanding of your financial amount is the first step towards financial independence.

3. What is your debt and the interest rate on your credit card?

You probably rarely calculate in cash. It is much more convenient to use cards or applications now. However, this can lead to overspending and getting into debt.

Do not forget to monitor the balance of your credit card every month. Set up SMS notifications or check the balance daily in the banking application. To maintain a good credit history, try not to spend more than 30% of your limit and repay the debt within a month.

If you are unable to pay off the credit card debt within a month, it is important to know how much interest will be charged to you. This will help you make a decision about which debt to repay first and which card to use in unexpected situations.

4. How much do you want to earn?

Do not forget that money is not the most important thing. However, your earnings depend on many factors that you cannot control. It is not worth dedicating your whole life to earning money. However, it is important to set goals and strive to achieve them.

Knowing the required amount makes it easier to make decisions about your career. Think about what you are working for. If you strive for financial independence, you need to earn and save a certain amount. This will allow you to retire earlier or start your own business. If you work to support your family, your income should be sufficient to meet all family needs.

Define the amount you aim for, but do not get too fixated on strict limits. It's okay if you don't reach your ideal amount down to the exact date. There are always factors that are beyond your control.
