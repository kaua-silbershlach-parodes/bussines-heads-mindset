# 5 golden rules for those heading towards success

![5 golden rules for those heading towards success](../images/060-5-golden-rules-for-those-heading-towards-success-04-13.jpg)

1. Never look back. Let go of your past.

2. Never treat time as if you have it forever. Always spend it wisely.

3. Never say more than necessary.

4. Never blame anyone for your mistakes and failures.

5. Never make vital decisions if you are in the mood. Both good and bad.
